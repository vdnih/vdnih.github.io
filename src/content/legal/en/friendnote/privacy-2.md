## How the information is used

Information is used only for the purposes listed in the table above. Specifically:

- To provide the app's functionality and sync your data across devices
- To respond when you contact support
- To investigate bugs and improve the quality of the app

Your information is never used for advertising, and it is never sold to third parties.

## Sharing with third parties

Apart from the services listed under "Third-party services" above, your information is not shared with anyone without your consent. The exceptions are:

- When disclosure is required by law
- When it is necessary to protect someone's life, health, or property and obtaining consent is not practical

## How long information is kept

Your information is kept for as long as your account exists, in order to provide the app's functionality. What happens when you delete your account is described below.

## Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and all data associated with it at any time — either from inside the app or by emailing a request. For the steps and how long deletion takes, see [Deleting your account and data](/en/apps/friendnote/delete-account).

## How information is protected

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored in cloud services with access controls in place. That said, no method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## International transfers

Because of how the underlying cloud services operate, your information may be stored on servers outside your country. In all cases it is used only within the scope set out in this policy.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. If it comes to light that information has been collected from a child under 13, that information will be deleted promptly.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, restriction of, or deletion of your information. To do so, use the contact details at the bottom of this page. Requests are handled within a reasonable period once your identity has been confirmed.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the law changes or as features are added to the app. Significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page. A revised policy takes effect when it is published here.
